'''Coulombs''' is a former [[Communes of France|commune]] in the [[Calvados (department)|Calvados]] [[Departments of France|department]] in the [[Normandy (administrative region)|Normandy]] [[Regions of France|region]] in northwestern [[France]]. On 1 January 2017, it was merged into the new commune [[Moulins en Bessin]].<ref>[https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/eli/arrete/2016/9/8/INTB1629491A/jo/texte Arrêté préfectoral] 8 September 2016 {{in lang|fr}}</ref>
