'''Sainte-Honorine-la-Chardonne''' is a [[Communes of France|commune]] in the [[Orne]] [[Departments of France|department]] in north-western [[France]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.insee.fr/fr/metadonnees/geographie/commune/61407-sainte-honorine-la-chardonne|title=Commune de Sainte-Honorine-la-Chardonne (61407)|publisher=INSEE}}</ref>

==Geography==

The commune is part of the area known as [[Norman Switzerland|Suisse Normande]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.office-tourisme-putanges.com/decouvrir/images/carte-suisse-normande/carte-sn.pdf|title=Map of Suisse Normande}}</ref>

The commune is made up of the following collection of villages and hamlets, '''La Barbotière''','''Cingal''', '''La Pouplière''', '''Les Petits Champs''','''La Soubinière''','''La Ferté''', '''Le Châtellier''', '''La Forgé Martin''', '''La Rivière''', '''La Pérée''', '''La Bernottière''', '''La Grésillière''', '''Laubenière''' and '''Sainte-Honorine-la-Chardonne'''.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.google.com/maps/place/61430+Sainte-Honorine-la-Chardonne,+France/@48.8233278,-0.5029888,14z/data=!3m1!4b1!4m6!3m5!1s0x480a25b9fe5d2541:0x40c14484fb80b90!8m2!3d48.824362!4d-0.486176!16s/m/03x_xt5?entry=ttu|title=Sainte-Honorine-la-Chardonne · 61430, France|website=Sainte-Honorine-la-Chardonne · 61430, France}}</ref>

The only two watercourses to run through the commune are [[La Vere]] and La Courteille.<ref>{{Sandre|id=I24-0410|nom=La Vere}}</ref><ref>{{Sandre|id=I2368000|nom=La Courteille}}</ref>

==Notable buildings and places==

'''Renaissance Garden of Sainte-Honorine-la-Chardonne''' is a 5000m2 garden built around the ruins of the Manoir de la Boisnerie and is open to the public.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.ouest-france.fr/normandie/sainte-honorine-la-chardonne-61430/sainte-honorine-la-chardonne-dix-ans-de-travail-dans-le-jardin-renaissance-recompenses-f44a7374-2dea-11ec-806e-9617b2a03500|title=Dix ans de travail récompensés au jardin Renaissance de Sainte-Honorine-la-Chardonne|date=October 17, 2021|website=Ouest-France.fr}}</ref> The garden was classed as a [[List of Remarkable Gardens of France|Jardins remarquables]] by the [[Ministry of Culture (France)|Ministry of Culture]] and the '''Comité des Parcs et Jardins de France''' in 2021.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.parcsetjardins.fr/jardins/1693-jardins-du-manoir-de-la-boisnerie|title=Jardins du manoir de La Boisnerie - Comité des Parcs et Jardins de France|website=www.parcsetjardins.fr}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=Sainte-Honorine-la-Chardonne – Jardin du manoir de la Boisnerie |url=https://monumentum.fr/jardin-remarquable/jar0000456/sainte-honorine-la-chardonne-jardin-du-manoir-de-la-boisnerie |website=Monumentum |publisher=Monumentum |language=fr |access-date=11 December 2025}}</ref>

===National heritage sites===

'''Château de Saint-Sauveur''' is a 16th Century Chateau that was classed as a [[Monument historique]] in 2009.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://monumentum.fr/monument-historique/pa00110925/sainte-honorine-la-chardonne-chateau-de-saint-sauveur|title=Château de Saint-Sauveur à Sainte-Honorine-la-Chardonne - PA00110925|website=monumentum.fr}}</ref>
