'''Longues-sur-Mer''' ({{IPA|fr|lɔ̃ɡ syʁ mɛʁ|-|Fr-Paris--Longues-sur-Mer.ogg}}) is a [[Communes of France|commune]] in the [[Calvados (department)|Calvados]] [[Departments of France|department]] in [[Normandy (administrative region)|Normandie]] [[Regions of France|region]] in northwestern [[France]].

The [[Longues-sur-Mer battery]] is nearby, part of the [[Atlantic Wall]] coastal fortifications.
