'''Martragny''' ({{IPA|fr|maʁtʁaɲi|-|Fr-Martragny.ogg}}) was a former [[Communes of France|commune]] in the [[Calvados (department)|Calvados]] [[Departments of France|department]] in the [[Normandy (administrative region)|Normandy]] [[Regions of France|region]] of northwestern [[France]]. On 1 January 2017, it was merged into the new commune of [[Moulins en Bessin]].<ref>[https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/eli/arrete/2016/9/8/INTB1629491A/jo/texte Arrêté préfectoral] 8 September 2016 {{in lang|fr}}</ref><ref>{{cite web | url=https://www.dday-overlord.com/en/battle-of-normandy/cities/martragny | title=Martragny in 1944 – Calvados – Battle of Normandy - D-Day Overlord | date=11 July 2016 }}</ref>
