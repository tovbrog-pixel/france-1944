**HEADQUARTERS, FIRST UNITED STATES ARMY**
USS *Augusta*, Bay of the Seine
060100B June 1944

FIELD ORDER No. 1 (game order for the Bradley role)

Maps: GSGS 4250, France 1:50,000; the game map.

Task organization: Annex A.

**1. INFORMATION**

a. *Enemy.* Annex B. The 352nd Division holds behind Omaha with the 716th on its right. The 709th and 243rd Divisions and the 91st Airlanding Division hold the Cotentin. The 17th SS Panzer Grenadier Division moves up from the Loire and reaches the Carentan front about 12 June.

b. *Friendly.* 21st Army Group Operations Order No. 1. Second British Army lands on our left.

**2. DECISION**

First Army lands on Omaha and Utah on 6 June, joins the two beachheads at Carentan, cuts the Cotentin and takes Cherbourg. It then attacks south through St-Lô to open the way for Third Army.

*Coordinating details.* On 1 August 1944 you take command of 12th Army Group: First Army (Lieutenant General Hodges) and Third Army (Lieutenant General Patton). Until 1 September you answer to General Montgomery for ground operations.

**3. TACTICAL MISSIONS**

a. *V Corps.* Lands the 1st and 29th Infantry Divisions on Omaha. Takes the Isigny-Bayeux road on D-Day and joins the British at Port-en-Bessin.

b. *VII Corps.* Lands the 4th Infantry Division on Utah, joins the 82nd and 101st Airborne Divisions, and takes Carentan to link with V Corps.

c. *Follow-up divisions.* Land over Omaha or Utah on their historical dates (Annex A).

d. *Breakout.* When St-Lô is taken, mass armor behind it for the breakout. Carpet bombing for a set-piece attack comes in a later build; in this build the bombing squadrons strike depots, bridges and airfields (Annex D).

e. *Your orders in play.* Draw lines of advance and objective lines for each division, attach corps troops from the **Support** tab, and request air support from the **Air** tab. A commander may refuse an order he cannot carry out and gives his reason.

x. *All divisions.* The bocage slows every advance and favors the defender. Expect short gains at high cost until the breakout.

**4. ADMINISTRATIVE MATTERS**

Annex F. Utah and Omaha beach dumps open from H+8; Cherbourg port once taken and cleared.

**5. SIGNAL COMMUNICATION**

Annex G.

BRADLEY
Lieutenant General

OFFICIAL:
G-3, First United States Army

Annexes: A to G (shared with all roles).

Distribution: 21st Army Group; V and VII Corps; the player.
