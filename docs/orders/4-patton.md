**HEADQUARTERS, THIRD UNITED STATES ARMY**
Nehou, Manche
011200B August 1944

FIELD ORDER No. 1 (game order for the Patton role)

Maps: GSGS 4250, France 1:50,000; the game map.

Task organization: Annex A.

**1. INFORMATION**

a. *Enemy.* Annex B. The German left has broken at Avranches. Brittany holds the 2nd Parachute and 343rd Divisions at Brest and weak static divisions at Lorient and St-Nazaire. Seventh Army is drawing armor from the Caen front toward Mortain.

b. *Friendly.* 12th Army Group order of 1 August. First Army holds the shoulder from Vire to Mortain.

**2. DECISION**

Third Army passes through Avranches, clears Brittany and its ports, and drives east toward Le Mans and the Seine.

*Coordinating details.* Until 1 August this role plays the Fortitude phase: the deception army in England that holds the Fifteenth Army at the Pas de Calais. In this build the Fortitude phase is not yet playable, and the role begins on 1 August.

**3. TACTICAL MISSIONS**

a. *VIII Corps.* Into Brittany: Rennes, then Brest, Lorient and St-Nazaire.

b. *XV Corps.* East toward Laval and Le Mans.

c. *XX and XII Corps.* Follow as they arrive and extend the drive toward Orléans and the Seine.

d. *Exceeding orders (later build).* You will be able to push past an objective to a depth set in the data. Success forgives the overreach; failure costs standing with your superior. Boundaries and axes stay fixed.

x. *All divisions.* Keep the Avranches corridor open. Every division passes through it.

**4. ADMINISTRATIVE MATTERS**

Annex F. Supply follows the army over the Avranches roads from the beaches and Cherbourg. Fuel runs short as the drive lengthens.

**5. SIGNAL COMMUNICATION**

Annex G.

PATTON
Lieutenant General

OFFICIAL:
G-3, Third United States Army

Annexes: A to G (shared with all roles).

Distribution: 12th Army Group; VIII, XII, XV and XX Corps; the player.
