**HEADQUARTERS, 21st ARMY GROUP**
Southwick Park, near Portsmouth
052200B June 1944

OPERATIONS ORDER No. 1 (game order for the Montgomery role)

Maps: GSGS 4250, France 1:50,000; the game map.

Task organization: Annex A.

**1. INFORMATION**

a. *Enemy.* Annex B. Expect the 21st Panzer Division to counterattack north of Caen on D-Day and the 12th SS Panzer Division to join it on 7 June. Panzer Lehr follows by 8 June near Tilly-sur-Seulles. Divisions from Brittany march on foot and arrive from 10 June. II SS Panzer Corps comes from the east in late June.

b. *Friendly.* SHAEF Operations Order No. 1 gives the mission. First US Army lands on the right, Second British Army on the left.

**2. DECISION**

21st Army Group assaults the Normandy coast between the Vire estuary and the Orne on 6 June. It takes Caen and Bayeux on the first day if it can, joins the beachheads, and clears the Cotentin to open Cherbourg. It then breaks out to Brittany and the Seine.

*Coordinating details.* H-Hour is 0630 on the US beaches, 0725 on Gold and Sword, and 0735 to 0745 on Juno. The airborne divisions land in the night before H-Hour on the flanks: the 82nd and 101st Airborne behind Utah, the 6th Airborne east of the Orne. First-day objectives appear as objective lines on the map (Annex C).

**3. TACTICAL MISSIONS**

a. *First US Army.* V Corps lands the 1st and 29th Infantry Divisions on Omaha and drives on the Isigny-Bayeux road. VII Corps lands the 4th Infantry Division on Utah and joins the 82nd and 101st Airborne Divisions at Ste-Mère-Église.

b. *Second British Army.* XXX Corps lands the 50th Division on Gold and takes Bayeux. I Corps lands the 3rd Canadian Division on Juno toward Carpiquet and the 3rd British Division on Sword toward Caen. The 6th Airborne Division holds the Orne bridges and the high ground east of the river.

c. *Follow-up divisions* land over the beach of the corps they join, on their historical dates (Annex A).

d. *Your orders in play.* Select a division, press **Draw line of advance** and drag the route, then draw the objective line and press **Issue order**. The card shows a staff estimate: arrival time, the odds, and the hours of fighting the division's supply covers. To order a whole corps, press **Order the whole corps** on the card of any of its divisions before you draw. The corps commander splits the objective line among his divisions from left to right and may keep one in reserve. Army orders come in a later build.

x. *All divisions.* Report contact. A commander who judges the odds too poor halts and says what he needs. Give him guns, air or a second division.

**4. ADMINISTRATIVE MATTERS**

Annex F. Divisions draw supply from the nearest open beach dump by truck column. A division cut off from every dump loses strength each day.

**5. SIGNAL COMMUNICATION**

Annex G. Your headquarters is the army group panel.

MONTGOMERY
General

OFFICIAL:
G-3 (Operations), 21st Army Group

Annexes: A to G (shared with all roles).

Distribution: SHAEF; First US Army; Second British Army; the player.
