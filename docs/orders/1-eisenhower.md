**SUPREME HEADQUARTERS, ALLIED EXPEDITIONARY FORCE**
Advanced Command Post, Southwick House, near Portsmouth
052000B June 1944

OPERATIONS ORDER No. 1 (game order for the Eisenhower role)

Maps: GSGS 4250, France 1:50,000; the game map, Brest to Paris, the Channel coast to the Loire.

Task organization: Annex A.

**1. INFORMATION**

a. *Enemy.* Annex B. Seventh Army holds Normandy and Brittany with static coast divisions and the 352nd Division behind Omaha. Panzer Group West keeps the 21st Panzer Division at Caen, with the 12th SS Panzer and Panzer Lehr Divisions within a night's march. Fifteenth Army holds the Pas de Calais. The German Air Force can put about 319 aircraft over the beaches on D-Day.

b. *Friendly.* The Combined Chiefs of Staff directed on 12 February 1944: "You will enter the continent of Europe and, in conjunction with the other United Nations, undertake operations aimed at the heart of Germany and the destruction of her armed forces." Over 3,500 gliders and 1,360 troop carrier and transport aircraft stand ready for the airborne assault. Naval forces U, O, G, J and S carry the assault divisions and give gunfire support.

**2. DECISION**

The Allied Expeditionary Force lands in Normandy on 6 June 1944, secures a lodgment containing Caen and Cherbourg, and expands it into Brittany and to the Seine by 1 September 1944.

*Coordinating details.* Victory goes by the points of the objectives held when the clock reaches 0000 hours 1 September 1944. Paris counts 40 points, Cherbourg and Caen 30, Brest 25. Every town of 10,000 people or more counts 10 or 20, and smaller road towns count 3. Annex C lists the objectives and gives the historical dates against which the game judges your pace.

**3. TACTICAL MISSIONS**

a. *21st Army Group* (General Montgomery) commands all Allied ground forces until 1 September 1944. It lands the assault and directs First US Army and Second British Army.

b. *First US Army* (Lieutenant General Bradley) passes to 12th Army Group on 1 August 1944, when Third Army (Lieutenant General Patton) becomes operational.

c. *Allied Expeditionary Air Force.* Annex D. You set the strategic bombers' targets until mid-September 1944: rail centers, bridges, depots and airfields.

d. *Your levers in this build.* You hold the theater reserve and the supply priority. You decide the timing of the airborne lifts and can hold or delay a lift. You may hand any formation to the staff.

x. *All commands.* Keep the beachheads joined. No formation crosses another's line of advance without an order. Report any objective lost within 30 minutes of game time.

**4. ADMINISTRATIVE MATTERS**

Annex F. The five beach dumps open from H+8 to H+9. Mulberry B at Arromanches opens on 16 June. Cherbourg port opens about 19 days after it is taken and cleared. The great storm of 19 to 22 June stops most discharge over the beaches.

**5. SIGNAL COMMUNICATION**

Annex G gives the controls. Your command post is the Supreme Headquarters panel; reports reach you in the box at the lower left, and the clock stops when a decision needs you.

EISENHOWER
General

OFFICIAL:
G-3
